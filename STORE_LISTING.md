## App Review Notes

This app performs facial analysis using on-device algorithms. No photos are uploaded to external servers. The analysis provides general aesthetic feedback and improvement suggestions based on commonly accepted facial proportions and grooming practices.

The app includes in-app purchases for premium features (unlimited scans, additional categories, progress tracking). A 3-day free trial is available.

All facial analysis is performed locally on the device. The app does not use facial recognition technology to identify individuals, nor does it store or transmit biometric data.

Demo account: Not required - the app works without any account creation.
