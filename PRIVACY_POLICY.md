# Privacy Policy for Androgenic

**Last Updated: February 15, 2026**

## Introduction

Androgenic ("we", "our", or "the app") is a face analysis application that provides personalized facial feature assessments and improvement recommendations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Photos and Camera Data
- **What we collect:** Photos you take or select for facial analysis
- **How we use it:** Photos are processed locally on your device to generate facial feature scores and recommendations
- **Storage:** All photo processing occurs on-device. Photos used for analysis are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload, transmit, or store your photos on any external servers.

### Usage Data
- **Analysis results:** Facial scores and improvement recommendations are stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage
- **Progress data:** If you use progress tracking, historical scores are stored locally on your device
- **Preferences:** App settings and onboarding preferences are stored locally

### Subscription Data
- **Purchase information:** Subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not collect or store payment information directly
- **Subscription status:** We store your subscription status locally to determine feature access

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** upload your photos to any server
- We do **not** use facial recognition to identify individuals
- We do **not** collect biometric data as defined under applicable privacy laws
- We do **not** sell, share, or transfer personal data to third parties
- We do **not** use analytics or tracking services
- We do **not** collect names, email addresses, or account information

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage (SecureStore/AsyncStorage)
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- You can delete all app data at any time through the app's Settings > Privacy & Security > Delete All Data option

## Children's Privacy

Androgenic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the app, please contact us.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access:** View all data stored by the app in Settings
- **Delete:** Remove all stored data through the app's privacy settings
- **Opt-out:** You can stop using the app and delete it at any time, which removes all locally stored data

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
- **Email:** privacy@androgenic.app
- **Website:** https://androgenic.app/privacy

## California Residents (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, California residents have additional rights. Since we do not collect, sell, or share personal information with third parties, most CCPA provisions do not apply. However, you retain all rights to request disclosure of any data practices.

## European Users (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, we process data based on legitimate interest (providing app functionality). Since all data is stored locally on your device and we do not transmit data externally, standard GDPR data transfer concerns do not apply. You maintain full control over your data at all times.
