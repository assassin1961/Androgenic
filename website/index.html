FAQ

Good questions, answered.

Is my photo private?

Yes. Analysis runs on-device and your photos are never uploaded to a server or shared. You can delete your history anytime.

How accurate is the analysis?

Our model detects dozens of facial landmarks and benchmarks them against established proportion research, reaching ~98% consistency on clear, front-facing photos. Lighting and angle matter — the app guides you to the best shot.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start scanning immediately. Everything is stored locally on your device.

What's the difference between Free and PRO?

Free gives you 3 scans and your core scores. PRO unlocks unlimited scans, all 8 categories, ratio analysis, the 12-week plan, progress tracking, the AI Advisor and the full LooksMax Hub.

Can I really improve my scores?

Absolutely. Skin, hair, body fat, posture, grooming and habits all move your scores — and they're exactly what your plan targets first. Track the gains week over week.
